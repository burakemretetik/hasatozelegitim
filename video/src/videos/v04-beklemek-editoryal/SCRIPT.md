# v04 — "Biraz daha bekleyelim mi?" · STYLE: Editorial / print magazine

**Topic:** Otizm – beklemek yerine harekete geçmek (blog: "Otizmde erken belirtiler", bölüm 5 ve kapanış)
**Target duration:** 40–50 s

## Style
A quiet, elegant **print-magazine essay** brought to life. Warm off-white paper with a very subtle
grain (SVG feTurbulence, low opacity), Fraunces serif — big italic pull quotes with hanging
punctuation, roman text set like a column, a drop cap, thin hairline rules, small-caps-like kicker
(written in literal capitals with letter-spacing), folio/page-number details, generous margins and
whitespace. Motion is slow and graceful: lines rise out of masks, a hand-drawn orange underline or
circle draws itself under the key phrase, subtle parallax between layers, page-turn or column-slide
transitions. Mostly cream/paper + green ink + one orange accent. The ending is a signature block.
It should feel like a page from a beautifully designed magazine — reflective and human.
