# v10 — Geçişleri kolaylaştırmak · STYLE: Bauhaus / Swiss geometric

**Topic:** Günlük rutinler – geçişler ve esneklik (blog: "Günlük rutinler çocuğunuza nasıl güven verir?", bölüm 3 ve 4)
**Target duration:** 42–52 s

## Style
**Bauhaus / Swiss modernism** in motion: a strict visible-or-implied grid, flat geometric primitives
(circle, square, triangle, semicircle, quarter-circle, bars), bold asymmetric compositions, big
Outfit typography (tight leading, strong weight contrast), numbers as graphic elements. Palette:
brand green, orange, cream + at most one supporting muted colour (e.g. a soft sage or a muted
brick/terracotta) and black-green. Motion is crisp and mechanical-elegant: shapes slide on the grid,
rotate in 90° steps, morph (circle → square), wipe, stack. Visual metaphors per beat:
- Hook: one shape (activity A) has to become another (activity B) — the morph "strains".
- Öneri 1: a big "5" with a pie/arc countdown that empties.
- Öneri 2: an hourglass built from two triangles with a stream of small circles flowing down.
- Öneri 3: the same shape/phrase repeated in a rhythmic pattern (identical repetition = predictability).
- Esneklik: a regular pattern of identical shapes where one shape gently changes colour/form while
  the grid stays stable.
Think a Bauhaus poster series that comes alive — but warm, not cold.
